Case studies

Case study 1 · Founder

Roovero: an AI content platform from idea to paying pilots

Dec 2025 – present · sole founder and product lead

Problem
Startups and small businesses need a steady stream of on-brand social content but lack the time and team to make it.
What I built
A platform that reads a company's website or PDFs, builds a brand profile (voice, colours, goals), plans a 30-day content calendar and generates posts, carousels and reels, with the customer approving content before it publishes.
How
Owned strategy, requirements and AI architecture, and built it myself with Claude Code and Codex: Flutter/Firebase app, Next.js site, routing across Claude Sonnet, Imagen, Veo and ElevenLabs, Instagram Graph API publishing and insights, subscription billing and webhooks.
Result
Launched with paying pilot clients. I use performance data and client feedback to keep tuning model routing, prompts and content strategy.
